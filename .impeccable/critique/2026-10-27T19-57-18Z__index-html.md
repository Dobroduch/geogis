## Overall Impression

The craft is real — the restrained one-accent system, the sequenced process-timeline reveal, and the hardened contact form are all genuinely well-built, and the code matches its own design documentation with unusual discipline. But the homepage's *content composition* hasn't caught up to the craft: every trust signal that would make a first-time visitor pick this specific firm over a competitor is parked in the footer, below a generic hero and an unreconciled service list. The single biggest opportunity is simple: the page already has the right facts (30 years, Holíč, real credentials) — it just needs to say them earlier.

## What's Working

1. **The 4-step process timeline's staggered, one-at-a-time reveal** — directly serves the product's "approachable for a first-timer" principle by concretely demystifying what happens after contact, and the sequenced (not simultaneous) motion makes it read as a guided walkthrough rather than decoration.
2. **The visual system matches its own documentation** — verified in the live CSS that breakpoints, the shadow vocabulary, and focus-visible states are implemented exactly as DESIGN.md specifies. That discipline is the right instinct for a long-established local firm.
3. **Contact form error handling is unusually robust for a framework-free static site** — honeypot, timing-based bot guard, `aria-live="polite"` status, and a failure message with a direct human fallback email rather than a dead end.
